### Ingredients

- 680g Skin-on salmon fillets — raw, thawed; individual fillets about 1 inch thick. Choose individual fillets about 170 g (6 oz) each, keeping the stated thickness.

- 454g Asparagus — trimmed, medium stalks

- 27g Olive oil — as purchased. Half to asparagus; half to salmon.

- 3g Garlic powder. Divide equally.

- 3g Fine salt. Use the proportional split described in the method.

- to taste Black pepper

- 2g Lemon zest — finely grated. Use finely grated zest.

- 30g Lemon juice — unsweetened

- 20g Hemp hearts — hulled. Use hulled hemp hearts in the measured portion.

### Equipment

- Two air fryers with room for four fillets in one basket and 454 g asparagus in the other

- Instant-read thermometer

### Cook

Prepare cold sides first. Keep raw-poultry tools separate; use clean serving utensils. Probe more than one thick piece. A timer is not a doneness test.

1. **Prepare.** Preheat both fryers to 390°F. Toss asparagus with half the oil, half the garlic, and half the salt. Spread in one basket without a dense pile.

2. **Cook.** Pat salmon dry. Rub with remaining oil, garlic, salt, pepper, and zest. Put skin-side down in the other basket with gaps between fillets. Start both together.

3. **Finish.** Check asparagus at 7 minutes; medium stalks usually need 7–10. Check salmon at 10 minutes; allow 10–14 to reach 145°F. Remove each when ready. Finish with lemon juice and hemp.

### Attention timeline

- Preheat and load: 6–8 min. Toss and load separate baskets; preheat overlaps prep.

- Air fry together: 7–14 min. Check asparagus at 7 min and salmon at 10 min; remove each separately.

- Finish: 2 min. Check fish at 145°F; add lemon and hemp.

Stages can overlap; endpoints and appliance behavior override the estimate.

### Flavor and capacity

Lemon brightens the salmon; hemp adds a mild nutty finish without another cooking step.

- Thin asparagus needs an earlier check.

- Keep the fillets skin-side down and apart.

If either basket cannot hold its full batch, use two half-sheet pans at 425°F: fish about 12–18 min, asparagus 10–15 min; allow 25–35 min total.

### Leftovers

### Ingredients

- 680g Boneless skinless chicken thighs — raw, thawed, unfolded; no more than ¾ inch thick

- 454g Broccoli florets — about 1½ inches across

- 27g Olive oil — as purchased. Half to broccoli; half to chicken.

- 2g Smoked paprika

- 3g Garlic powder

- ½ tsp Dried oregano

- 5g Fine salt. Use the proportional split described in the method.

- to taste Black pepper

- 15g Lemon juice — unsweetened

- 8g Nutritional yeast — flakes. Add after cooking.

### Equipment

- Two air fryers with uncrowded baskets

- Instant-read thermometer

- Separate raw-poultry bowl and clean serving tools

### Cook

Prepare cold sides first. Keep raw-poultry tools separate; use clean serving utensils. Probe more than one thick piece. A timer is not a doneness test.

1. **Prepare.** Preheat both fryers to 390°F. Toss broccoli with half the oil and one third of the salt; load one basket without crowding. In a separate bowl coat chicken with the remaining oil and salt, paprika, garlic, oregano, and pepper.

2. **Cook.** Put unfolded thighs smooth-side up in the other basket without overlapping. Start chicken. Five minutes later, start broccoli.

3. **Finish.** Shake broccoli once halfway through its 10–14-minute cook. Chicken usually needs 16–22 minutes; check the thickest pieces for 165°F. No chicken flip is required, though the underside browns less. Finish broccoli with lemon and nutritional yeast. Broccoli is ready when stems are fork-tender and crowns are lightly browned; time alone is not its endpoint.

### Attention timeline

- Preheat and load chicken: 7–9 min. Start chicken first; preheat overlaps prep.

- Start broccoli: 5 min after chicken. Load broccoli basket.

- Cook: Chicken 16–22 min; broccoli 10–14 min. Shake broccoli once halfway; check chicken thickest pieces for 165°F.

- Finish: 2 min. Add lemon and yeast after cooking.

Stages can overlap; endpoints and appliance behavior override the estimate.

### Flavor and capacity

Smoked paprika and oregano season the thighs while lemon and yeast sharpen the broccoli after cooking.

- Chicken underside browns less without a flip.

- Keep yeast out of the fryer to avoid scorching.

If baskets cannot hold the batch, use two oven pans at 425°F for about 20–25 min; allow 35–40 min total and check chicken for 165°F.

317 kcal; 37.9 g protein; 9.5 g total carbs; 3.8 g fiber; 5.7 g net carbs

### Ingredients

- 680g Cod fillets — raw, thawed; individual fillets about 1 inch thick. Choose individual fillets about 170 g (6 oz) each, keeping the stated thickness.

- 680g Zucchini — sliced into ½-inch half-moons

- 45g DeLallo basil pesto — squeeze-bottle, as sold. Weigh the scaled amount; do not infer tablespoons from the unusual label volume. Substitute at most 2 g net carbs per 30 g.

- 27g Olive oil — as purchased. Three quarters to zucchini; remainder to fish.

- 2g Fine salt. Use the measured salt in the zucchini seasoning.

- to taste Black pepper

- 15g Lemon juice — unsweetened

- 20g Hemp hearts — hulled. Use hulled hemp hearts in the measured portion.

### Equipment

- Oven at 425°F on bake

- Two 18×13-inch rimmed metal half-sheet pans

- Instant-read thermometer

### Cook

Prepare cold sides first. Keep raw-poultry tools separate; use clean serving utensils. Probe more than one thick piece. A timer is not a doneness test.

1. **Prepare.** Heat the oven to 425°F. Toss zucchini with three quarters of the oil, salt, and pepper. Spread on one half-sheet pan. Pat fish dry, rub with the remaining oil, and place on the other pan.

2. **Cook.** Start the zucchini pan first. After 6 minutes, add the fish pan. Check zucchini from 18 minutes of its own cooking time for browned edges and a tender center; allow about 18–23 minutes. Check fish after 12 minutes of its own cooking time; allow about 12–18 minutes to reach 145°F. Remove each pan at its endpoint and serve promptly. If either needs longer, keep checking; do not overcook the fish to match the vegetables.

3. **Finish.** Spoon pesto over cooked fish. Finish fish and zucchini with lemon and hemp. Applying pesto after cooking keeps its basil flavor and avoids burning the sauce.

### Attention timeline

- Preheat and load zucchini: 10–12 min. Preheat overlaps prep; fish and zucchini get separate pans.

- Give zucchini a head start: 6 min. Add fish pan after 6 minutes: one scheduled main-appliance action.

- Finish both pans: Fish 12–18 min; zucchini 18–23 min total roast. Check zucchini for browned edges and tenderness; fish must reach 145°F. Remove separately at their endpoints.

- Finish: 2 min. Spoon pesto on fish; add lemon and hemp and serve promptly.

Stages can overlap; endpoints and appliance behavior override the estimate.

### Flavor and capacity

Separating wet zucchini keeps cod from steaming in vegetable liquid; pesto stays fresh when added after heat.

- The six-minute vegetable head start reduces the fish holding time; doneness still decides when each pan comes out.

- Pesto goes on after cooking to protect basil flavor.

### Nutrition per serving

328 kcal; 33.9 g protein; 7.1 g total carbs; 2.3 g fiber; 4.8 g net carbs

Per adult: base includes measured sauces, seeds and sides. Family nutrition adds one selected starch portion. US net carbs = total carbohydrate − fiber. Labels and food variation matter; no guarantee of ketosis.

Known allergens for these plates: fish, milk, tree-nuts. Check product labels and cross-contact.

### First-cook checks and limits

- Two-pans and conventional bake are assumed.

- Pesto may contain tree nuts or other allergens; verify the specific jar label. This data is not an allergy guarantee.

- The six-minute head start is a design adjustment, not a tested synchronization guarantee.
